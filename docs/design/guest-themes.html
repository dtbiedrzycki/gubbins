The Gubbins guest view: Storybook

The guest screens as built for launch. Tap “Hold this item” on the list to see the one bit of motion: the seal stamping in under “Your holds”.

Storybook

The list reads like a page from a picture book and sits behind a round green door. The whimsy lives in the drawings, the type and the key screen’s greeting. Buttons and errors say exactly what they do.

The bold thing

Wax seals. A held gift carries a still wax-red seal, a purchased one a gold seal. A new hold’s seal stamps in once under “Your holds”. Group gifts fill a wishing jar with gold coins.

Palette

Type

IM Fell English for headings and, in italic, the status line. Alegreya Sans for prices, buttons and everything else. Both are self-hosted.

Wording

  • Key screen: “Knock, knock.” and “Enter the guest key from your invite.”
  • List header: “When you hold an item, it’s held for you for 3 days.”
  • Held: “<title> is held.”
  • Purchased: “<title> has been purchased.”
