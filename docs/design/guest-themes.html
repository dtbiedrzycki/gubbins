Three looks for the Gubbins guest view

Each direction takes a different reading of “gubbins”: the bits and bobs a household runs on. Same screens, same words on the buttons, same flow. Tap “Hold this item” on any list to see that theme’s one bit of motion.

Parts bin

The wishlist is a hardware-store cabinet of small drawers, one per gift. Each title sits in a little label slot. A held gift is a drawer that’s been shut and tagged.

The bold thing

Drawer fronts with label slots and pulls. Everything else is flat steel and plain type.

Palette

Type

Barlow Condensed for drawer labels, like stamped shelf signage. Atkinson Hyperlegible for everything you read.

Voice

  • Locked screen: “This cabinet’s locked.”
  • Held tag: “Spoken for”
  • Footer: the dictionary entry for gubbins.

Most practical of the three. Easiest to keep consistent as the owner view arrives.

Storybook

Gubbins is the name of the hobgoblin who keeps the house. Folklore’s household brownies tidy up while everyone sleeps. The list reads like a page from a picture book, opens with “Once upon a list”, and sits behind a round green door.

The bold thing

Wax seals. Holding a gift stamps a seal next to it, and the seal lands with a little thump. Group gifts fill a wishing jar with gold coins.

Palette

Type

IM Fell English, recut from 17th-century types, for titles and the storybook lines. Alegreya Sans for prices, buttons and everything you have to act on.

Voice

  • Locked screen: “Knock, knock.”
  • Held seal: “Promised to someone”, or “Promised to you”
  • Bought: “Found its home”
  • Footer: “Kept by Gubbins, a hobgoblin of tidy habits.”

Most charming of the three. The storybook voice stays in headings and statuses, so buttons still say exactly what they do.

Clear case

Gubbins as a gadget’s insides: the page sits on a faint circuit board, seen through frosted lilac plastic like a see-through 90s handheld. Statuses are status lights.

The bold thing

The board showing through the frosted case. Green light means available, amber means held. Group gifts fill a row of segment lights.

Palette

Type

Unbounded for the list title and section heads. Figtree for everything else.

Voice

  • Locked screen: “Plug in your guest key.”
  • Light labels: “Available”, “Held”, “Bought”
  • Footer: “All the gubbins, none of the guesswork.”

Most distinctive. Needs care on cheap phones, where frosted glass can be slow.
