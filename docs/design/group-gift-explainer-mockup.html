How holding a gift works

  1. Hold it before you buy it. Other guests see it’s taken, so nobody buys it twice.
  2. Buy it within 14 days. The hold is yours until then, and you can extend it once.
  3. Come back and mark it purchased. If you don’t, the hold ends after 14 days and another guest can buy the same gift.

How group gifts work

  1. Chip in any amount. Guests pool money toward one bigger gift.
  2. Send it straight to the host. When you chip in, we’ll show you how to pay the host with Venmo or Zelle.
  3. The host buys the gift. They use what everyone sent to buy it.
