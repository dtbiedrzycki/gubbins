# NNNN. Short title of the decision

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** names / roles

## Context

What is the situation? What forces are at play — technical, product,
team, time? What makes a decision necessary now? State constraints and
assumptions plainly.

## Decision

The choice, in the active voice: "We will …". Be specific enough that
someone can act on it without asking follow-up questions.

## Alternatives considered

- **Option A** — why it was rejected.
- **Option B** — why it was rejected.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we now have to maintain
or revisit. Include follow-up work and anything this decision commits us
to.
